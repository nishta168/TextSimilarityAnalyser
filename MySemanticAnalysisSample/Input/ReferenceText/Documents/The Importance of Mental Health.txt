Mental health is an integral part of overall well-being, yet it is often overlooked or stigmatized. Just like physical health, mental health requires attention and care. Conditions like anxiety, depression, and stress can affect anyone, regardless of age or background, and can impact daily life, relationships, and work. Taking steps to maintain mental health, such as seeking therapy, practicing self-care, and staying connected with others, is essential for leading a fulfilling life. Reducing the stigma around mental health is crucial to creating a supportive environment where individuals feel comfortable seeking help and prioritizing their emotional well-being.
