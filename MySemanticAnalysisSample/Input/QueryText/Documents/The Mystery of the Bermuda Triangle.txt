The Bermuda Triangle, an area between Florida, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico, has fascinated conspiracy theorists for decades. Countless ships and planes have disappeared under mysterious circumstances, leading to wild theories about alien abductions, underwater pyramids, and magnetic anomalies. However, scientists suggest that natural explanations, such as unpredictable weather and strong ocean currents, are the real culprits. Despite the myths, the Bermuda Triangle remains one of the world’s most intriguing unsolved mysteries.
