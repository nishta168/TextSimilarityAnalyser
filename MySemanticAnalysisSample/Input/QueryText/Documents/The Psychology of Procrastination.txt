Everyone has procrastinated at some point, but why do we delay tasks even when we know they are important? Psychologists suggest that procrastination isn't just about laziness—it’s often linked to emotions like anxiety, perfectionism, or fear of failure. People tend to avoid tasks that seem overwhelming, choosing short-term comfort over long-term benefits. However, breaking tasks into smaller steps and setting deadlines can help overcome procrastination. Understanding the root cause of this habit can lead to better productivity and reduced stress.
