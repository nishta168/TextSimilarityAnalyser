Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming industries, from healthcare to finance, by automating tasks and improving efficiency. AI-powered chatbots, self-driving cars, and personalized recommendations are just a few examples of how this technology is reshaping our world. However, ethical concerns, such as data privacy and job displacement, remain major topics of discussion. As AI continues to advance, striking a balance between innovation and responsible use will be crucial for its long-term impact on society.
