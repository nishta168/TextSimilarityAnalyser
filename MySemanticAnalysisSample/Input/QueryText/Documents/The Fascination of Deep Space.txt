Space has always been a source of wonder and mystery, but deep space—far beyond our solar system—holds some of the most intriguing phenomena. Scientists use powerful telescopes like the James Webb Space Telescope to observe exoplanets, distant galaxies, and cosmic events like black holes merging. These discoveries help us understand the origins of the universe and whether life might exist beyond Earth. The vastness of space reminds us how much is yet to be explored and how small we are in the grand scheme of things.
