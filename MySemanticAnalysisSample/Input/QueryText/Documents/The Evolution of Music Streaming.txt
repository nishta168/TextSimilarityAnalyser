Gone are the days when people bought CDs or downloaded individual songs. Today, music streaming services like Spotify and Apple Music have changed how we listen to music, offering millions of tracks at our fingertips. Algorithms personalize playlists, introducing users to new artists based on their tastes. While this convenience benefits listeners, it has also changed the way musicians earn, with debates about fair compensation from streaming revenue. The digital music revolution continues to reshape the industry, making music more accessible than ever.
